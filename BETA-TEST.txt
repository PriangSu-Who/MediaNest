MediaNest v0.1.0-beta.1

First end-to-end beta test build.

Start:
1. Double-click MediaNest.cmd.
2. If components are missing, choose the setup option.
3. Let setup download and verify yt-dlp, Deno, and FFmpeg.
4. After setup, test Video, MP3, and Playlist downloads.

This beta intentionally uses the current tested MediaNest v1.2.3 download
architecture and Step A structured progress output. The new Step B visual
progress bar is not included yet.
